Q. Why computers understand only binary numbers
* 8 bits form -> 1 byte

- the weights in python will be converted to binary or hexadecimal because fpga can read only these type of values.
- 2's compliment is used to find negative value of a number.
    2's compliment is great for signed arithmetic operations.

- Q8.8 means we divide 16 bits into -> 8 integer part and 8 fraction part
    for Q8.8 scale = 2^8 = 256

- now the question might arise that why did we scale weights and biases by 256 in the initial phase if we wanted it back in decimal.
So the simple answer is that fpga can only calculate in integers, so first we convert to fixed point integers by scaling them by 256.
Then we bring it back to decimals just for our personal understandind and debugging.

- after multiplying in MAC, we shift the product by 8 bits because 256 is multiplied 2 times.
    since 256 = 2^8, so to divide we shift right.
    and we use arithmetic shift to preserve the sign, if we use logical shift then zeroes will keep adding on left.

- the hardware block has to take input of weights and biases in Q8.8, then multiply them, it gives a 32-bit product which is converted to 8 bits
    by shifted right by 8 bits which gives the final output.
